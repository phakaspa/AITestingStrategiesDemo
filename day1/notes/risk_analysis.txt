Hallucination Risk

Description:
The model generates incorrect information.

Business Impact:
Users may lose trust in the application.

------------------------------------------------

Relevance Risk

Description:
The response does not answer the user's question.

Business Impact:
Poor customer experience.

------------------------------------------------

Completeness Risk

Description:
Important details are missing.

Business Impact:
Reduced usefulness.

------------------------------------------------

Safety Risk

Description:
The response contains harmful or offensive language.

Business Impact:
Compliance issues and brand damage.
